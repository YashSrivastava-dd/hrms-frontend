## Getting Started

Welcome to the HRMS system! This guide will walk you through how to use the interface based on your role. The system has four main user types:

- **👤 Employee**: Personal dashboard, leave requests, attendance tracking
- **👥 Manager**: Team oversight, leave approvals, team management
- **🏢 HR Admin**: Employee management, payroll, system administration
- **👑 CEO/Super-Admin**: Executive dashboard, organizational analytics

## Logging In & Out

### How to Log In

1. **Open the Application**
   - Go to the HRMS website URL
   - You'll see the login page with a clean, modern interface

2. **Enter Your Details**
   - Type your email address in the "Email" field
   - Enter your password in the "Password" field
   - Check "Remember Me" if you want to save your login details
   - Click the eye icon to show/hide your password

3. **Click Login**
   - Press the blue "Login" button
   - If details are correct, you'll see a success message
   - The system will automatically take you to your dashboard

4. **What Happens Next**
   - You'll land on your personalized dashboard
   - The left sidebar will show options based on your role
   - Your name and profile picture will appear in the top-right corner

### How to Log Out

**Method 1: Using the Profile Menu**
1. Click on your profile picture/name in the top-right corner
2. A dropdown menu will appear
3. Click "Logout" from the menu
4. Confirm by clicking "Logout" in the popup

**Method 2: Using Browser Back Button**
1. Click the browser's back button
2. A confirmation popup will appear asking if you want to leave
3. Click "Logout" to confirm or "Stay Here" to continue

**Method 3: Automatic Logout**
- The system will automatically log you out after a period of inactivity
- You'll see a warning message before this happens

## Employee Experience

### Your Dashboard
When you log in as an employee, you'll see:
- **Personal greeting** with your name
- **Leave balance cards** showing your available leaves
- **Today's attendance** status and punch in/out button
- **Recent announcements** from the company
- **Quick action buttons** for common tasks

### Marking Your Attendance

#### Punching In (Starting Your Day)
1. **Look for the Yellow Button**
   - In the top navigation bar, you'll see a yellow "Punch In" button
   - This appears when you haven't punched in yet

2. **Click Punch In**
   - Click the yellow button
   - Your camera will open automatically
   - The system will ask for camera permission - click "Allow"

3. **Take Your Photo**
   - Position yourself in the camera frame
   - Click "Capture" to take your selfie
   - The system uses this for attendance verification

4. **Allow Location Access**
   - The system will ask for your location
   - Click "Allow" when prompted
   - This helps verify you're at the right location

5. **Review and Submit**
   - You'll see your captured photo and detected location
   - If everything looks correct, click "Upload Image"
   - The button will change to red "Punch Out"

#### Punching Out (Ending Your Day)
1. **Look for the Red Button**
   - The yellow button changes to red "Punch Out" after you punch in
   - Click this when you're ready to end your work day

2. **Confirm Punch Out**
   - Click the red "Punch Out" button
   - The system will process your punch out
   - The button will disappear, showing your work session is complete

### Managing Your Leaves

#### Applying for Leave
1. **Go to Leaves Section**
   - Click "Leaves" in the left sidebar menu
   - You'll see a form to apply for leave

2. **Choose Leave Type**
   - Select from the dropdown: Earned Leave (EL), Casual Leave (CL), Medical Leave (ML), or Comp-off
   - Each type has different rules and balances

3. **Pick Your Dates**
   - Click on the calendar to select start date
   - Click again to select end date
   - The system will calculate total days automatically

4. **Explain Your Reason**
   - Type a clear reason for your leave in the text box
   - Be specific - this helps your manager understand

5. **Upload Documents (if needed)**
   - For medical leave, click "Choose File" to upload medical certificates
   - Supported formats: PDF, JPG, PNG, DOC files

6. **Review and Submit**
   - Check all your details are correct
   - Click the blue "Apply Leave" button
   - You'll see a green success message

#### Checking Leave Status
1. **Go to Leave Status**
   - Click "Leave Status" in the sidebar
   - You'll see all your leave requests in a table

2. **Filter Your Requests**
   - Use the dropdown to filter by status: All, Pending, Approved, or Rejected
   - Use the search box to find specific requests

3. **View Details**
   - Click on any leave request to see full details
   - You can see when it was applied, current status, and manager comments

4. **Take Actions**
   - For pending requests, you can delete them if needed
   - For approved leaves, you can request to revert (cancel) them

#### Comp-off Leave (Weekend Work)
1. **Select Comp-off Type**
   - In the leave form, choose "Comp-off" from the dropdown
   - This is for when you work on weekends

2. **Choose Weekend Date**
   - Select the weekend date you worked
   - Pick Saturday or Sunday from the calendar

3. **Enter Work Hours**
   - Type how many hours you worked
   - 1+ hours = half day comp-off
   - 4+ hours = full day comp-off

4. **Submit Request**
   - Click "Apply Leave" to submit
   - Your manager will review and approve

### Viewing Documents

#### Company Documents
1. **Go to Issued Documents**
   - Click "Issued Documents" in the sidebar
   - You'll see a list of company documents

2. **Browse Documents**
   - Scroll through the list to find what you need
   - Documents are organized by category

3. **Preview or Download**
   - Click on any document to preview it in a popup
   - Click the download button to save it to your computer

### Checking Your Payslips

1. **Go to Payroll Section**
   - Click "Payroll and Payslip" in the sidebar
   - You'll see your payslip history

2. **Select Month**
   - Use the dropdown to choose which month's payslip you want
   - Available months will be listed

3. **View Your Payslip**
   - Click "View" to see your detailed payslip
   - You can see all salary components, deductions, and net pay

4. **Download Payslip**
   - Click the download button to save the payslip as PDF
   - The file will be saved with your name and month

### Updating Your Profile

1. **Go to Profile**
   - Click "Profile" in the sidebar
   - You'll see your personal information

2. **Edit Your Details**
   - Click the "Edit" button to make changes
   - Update any fields you need to change

3. **Save Changes**
   - Click "Save" to update your information
   - You'll see a confirmation message

## Manager Experience

### Your Manager Dashboard
When you log in as a manager, you'll see:
- **Team overview** with statistics about your team
- **Pending requests** that need your attention
- **Quick action buttons** for common tasks
- **Team performance** metrics and analytics

### Managing Your Team

#### Viewing Team Members
1. **Go to Teammate's Profile**
   - Click "Teammate's Profile" in the sidebar
   - You'll see a list of all your team members

2. **Search and Filter**
   - Use the search box to find specific team members
   - Filter by department or designation using the dropdown
   - Sort by name, department, or other criteria

3. **View Team Member Details**
   - Click on any team member's name to see their full profile
   - You can see their contact info, leave balance, and work details

#### Monitoring Team Attendance
1. **Go to Attendance Section**
   - Click "Attendance" in the sidebar
   - You'll see attendance records for all team members

2. **Filter by Date Range**
   - Use the date picker to select a specific time period
   - View daily, weekly, or monthly attendance patterns

3. **Export Data**
   - Click "Export" to download attendance data as Excel file
   - Useful for reporting and analysis

### Approving Leave Requests

#### Viewing Pending Requests
1. **Go to Team Approvals**
   - Click "Team Approvals" in the sidebar
   - You'll see all pending requests from your team

2. **Filter Requests**
   - Use tabs to switch between different types: Leave, Comp-off, Vendor Meetings, Revert
   - Filter by date range or employee name
   - Search for specific requests

#### Approving a Leave Request
1. **Review the Request**
   - Click on any pending leave request
   - Check employee details, dates, reason, and any uploaded documents
   - Verify the employee's leave balance

2. **Approve the Request**
   - Click the green "Approve" button
   - The system will show a success message
   - The employee will be notified automatically

#### Rejecting a Leave Request
1. **Review the Request**
   - Click on the leave request you want to reject
   - Check all details before making your decision

2. **Reject with Reason**
   - Click the red "Reject" button
   - A popup will appear asking for a rejection reason
   - Type a clear explanation for the rejection
   - Click "Submit" to confirm

3. **Employee Notification**
   - The employee will receive a notification about the rejection
   - They can see your reason in their leave status

#### Handling Comp-off Requests
1. **Go to Comp-off Tab**
   - In Team Approvals, click the "Comp-off" tab
   - You'll see requests for weekend work compensation

2. **Review Weekend Work**
   - Check the date and hours worked
   - Verify if the work was necessary and properly documented
   - Ensure the hours match the comp-off request

3. **Approve or Reject**
   - Use the same approve/reject process as regular leave
   - Consider business needs and team workload

#### Managing Vendor Meeting Requests
1. **Go to Vendor Meetings Tab**
   - Click "Vendor Meetings" in Team Approvals
   - Review external meeting requests

2. **Check Meeting Details**
   - Verify the meeting purpose and duration
   - Ensure it's business-related and necessary
   - Check if it conflicts with other team activities

3. **Process the Request**
   - Approve if the meeting is justified
   - Reject if it's not necessary or conflicts with work

#### Handling Leave Revert Requests
1. **Go to Revert Tab**
   - Click "Revert" in Team Approvals
   - You'll see requests to cancel approved leaves

2. **Review Revert Reason**
   - Check why the employee wants to cancel their leave
   - Consider if it affects team planning
   - Verify the number of days to be reverted

3. **Approve or Reject Revert**
   - Approve if the revert makes sense
   - Reject if it would disrupt team operations

### Bulk Operations (For Multiple Requests)

#### Bulk Approve
1. **Select Multiple Requests**
   - Check the boxes next to multiple leave requests
   - You can select requests from the same page

2. **Click Bulk Approve**
   - Click the "Bulk Approve" button
   - Confirm your action in the popup
   - All selected requests will be approved at once

#### Bulk Reject
1. **Select Multiple Requests**
   - Check the boxes next to requests you want to reject
   - Make sure they all have similar rejection reasons

2. **Click Bulk Reject**
   - Click the "Bulk Reject" button
   - Enter a common rejection reason
   - All selected requests will be rejected with the same reason

## HR Admin Experience

### Your HR Admin Dashboard
When you log in as HR Admin, you'll see:
- **Company-wide statistics** and employee counts
- **Quick action buttons** for adding employees and creating announcements
- **Recent leave requests** that need attention
- **Employee analytics** and department breakdowns

### Managing Employees

#### Adding a New Employee
1. **Click Add Employee**
   - On your dashboard, click the "Add Employee" button
   - A detailed form will open in a popup

2. **Fill Personal Details**
   - Enter employee ID, name, and employee code
   - Select gender, department, and designation
   - Choose date of joining and employment type
   - Set employee status and account status

3. **Add Contact Information**
   - Enter father's and mother's names
   - Add residential and permanent addresses
   - Input contact number, email, and date of birth
   - Add place of birth and blood group

4. **Enter Work Details**
   - Specify work place and add Aadhaar number
   - Enter PAN card number and master device ID
   - Select marital status and nationality
   - Add overall experience and qualifications

5. **Set Emergency Contact**
   - Add emergency contact person's details
   - Include their relationship and phone number

6. **Assign Management**
   - Select manager and team lead from dropdowns
   - Set initial leave balances for the employee
   - Choose the employee's role in the system
   - Set shift time and working days

7. **Create Login Access**
   - Set a temporary password for the employee
   - The employee can change this after first login

8. **Submit Registration**
   - Review all information carefully
   - Click "Submit" to complete employee registration
   - The employee will receive login credentials

#### Managing Existing Employees
1. **Go to All Employees**
   - Click "All Employees" in the sidebar
   - You'll see a list of all employees in the company

2. **Search and Filter**
   - Use the search box to find specific employees
   - Filter by department, status, or other criteria
   - Sort by name, department, or joining date

3. **Edit Employee Information**
   - Click the "Edit" button next to any employee
   - Make necessary changes to their information
   - Click "Save" to update the details

### Managing Leave Requests

#### Viewing All Leave Requests
1. **Go to Employee Leave Status**
   - Click "Employee Leave Status" in the sidebar
   - You'll see all leave requests from all employees (245+ pages)

2. **Navigate Through Pages**
   - Use the pagination controls at the bottom
   - Click page numbers or "Next/Previous" buttons
   - Each page shows multiple leave requests

3. **Filter and Search**
   - Use the status dropdown to filter by Pending/Approved/Rejected
   - Search by employee name or designation
   - Filter by date range or leave type

#### Processing Leave Requests
1. **Review Request Details**
   - Click on any leave request to see full details
   - Check employee information, dates, and reason
   - View any uploaded medical certificates

2. **Approve or Reject**
   - Click "Approve" for valid requests
   - Click "Reject" and provide a reason for invalid requests
   - The employee will be notified of your decision

3. **Bulk Operations**
   - Select multiple requests using checkboxes
   - Use "Bulk Approve" or "Bulk Reject" for efficiency
   - Enter common reasons for bulk rejections

### Managing Payroll

#### Generating Salary Slips
1. **Go to Generate Salary Slip**
   - Click "Generate Salary Slip" in the sidebar
   - You'll see a comprehensive form

2. **Search for Employee**
   - Enter the employee ID in the search box
   - Click "Search" to load employee data
   - The form will auto-populate with employee details

3. **Select Month**
   - Choose the payslip month from the dropdown
   - Available months will be listed

4. **Enter Company Information**
   - Verify the company address
   - Update if needed

5. **Review Employee Details**
   - Check employee name, code, designation
   - Verify joining date, PAN, Aadhaar
   - Confirm bank details and UAN/ESIC numbers

6. **Enter Leave Summary**
   - Input month days, unpaid days, payable days
   - Enter leave taken (EL, CL, ML, Comp-off)
   - Add absent days and worked days

7. **Set Salary Details**
   - Enter gross salary amount
   - The system will auto-calculate basic, HRA, allowances
   - Add deductions (PF, ESI, TDS, etc.)
   - Review the calculated net pay

8. **Preview and Generate**
   - Click "Preview Payslip" to see how it looks
   - Make any final adjustments
   - Click "Generate Salary Slip" to save

### Creating Announcements

1. **Click Create Announcement**
   - On your dashboard, click "Create Announcement"
   - A form will open in a popup

2. **Write Your Announcement**
   - Enter a clear, descriptive title
   - Write the announcement content
   - Use formatting options if needed

3. **Set Announcement Details**
   - Choose the announcement category
   - Set priority level (High, Medium, Low)
   - Select publication date

4. **Publish the Announcement**
   - Review your announcement
   - Click "Publish" to make it live
   - All employees will see it on their dashboards

### Managing Documents

1. **Go to Issued Documents**
   - Click "Issued Documents" in the sidebar
   - You'll see all company documents

2. **Upload New Documents**
   - Click "Upload Document" button
   - Choose file from your computer
   - Add document title and description
   - Set access permissions

3. **Organize Documents**
   - Create categories for different document types
   - Move documents to appropriate categories
   - Set who can access each document

### Viewing Analytics

1. **Dashboard Analytics**
   - Your dashboard shows key metrics
   - View total employees, new hires, department breakdowns
   - See leave statistics and approval rates

2. **Employee Reports**
   - Access detailed employee reports
   - Generate attendance summaries
   - Create custom reports for management

3. **Export Data**
   - Export employee data to Excel
   - Download attendance reports
   - Generate payslip summaries

## Help & Support

### Common Problems and Solutions

#### Login Issues
- **Can't Remember Password**: Click "Forgot Password" on login page
- **Account Locked**: Contact HR department to unlock your account
- **Wrong Email**: Double-check your email address
- **Browser Problems**: Try clearing your browser cache and cookies

#### Attendance Problems
- **Camera Not Working**: Check if you've allowed camera access
- **Location Not Found**: Enable location services in your browser
- **Already Punched In**: You need to punch out before punching in again
- **Network Issues**: Check your internet connection

#### Leave Application Issues
- **No Leave Balance**: Check your available leave balance
- **Date Conflicts**: Make sure your leave dates don't overlap
- **File Upload Failed**: Check file size and format
- **Waiting for Approval**: Be patient - managers will review your request

#### Document Problems
- **Can't Download**: Check if you have permission to access the document
- **Preview Not Working**: Try using a different browser
- **Upload Failed**: Make sure file size is under the limit
- **Access Denied**: Contact HR for document permissions
